**Duration:** 3 minutes, 2 seconds. The video has on-screen captions and no audio. It records a synthetic local preview: opening a job, reviewing the bounded Critic revision and portfolio evidence, approving one synthetic draft, preparing and approving a synthetic client reply, and observing scam and prompt-injection skips. No real marketplace action occurs.

The instructor's example starter kit did not contain a working GigSim or gold evaluation data. This demonstration therefore cannot establish GigSim reply or hire performance. The [technical write-up](TECHNICAL_WRITEUP.html) and [data card](https://github.com/FazalZamanKhan/gigbid/blob/main/docs/DATA_CARD.md) explain that boundary.
